# CRUDBuilder



## Purpose

<!-- Clearly state the main idea and intended purpose of your application. -->

<!-- Explain a problem your application aims to solve and why it is important. -->

The main idea and purpose of our application is to aid offer a service for users to build and maintain a deck of cards from the popular game, Magic The Gathering. We plan to offer the ability of our users to create a multitude of decks for differing formats to their specification. The application will also aid users in the discovery of cards, using an API of a popular site, Scryfall. The search feature of our application will allow users to discover new cards and add them into their decks. This application will tend to the needs of deck builders to help them organize and enjoy their hobby.


## Users

<!-- Identify the primary users of your application, including their background or needs.-->

<!-- Justify how your application will benefit these users and improve their experience.-->

	Our users will be those that enjoy the hobby of Magic The Gathering. The background of our users will be varied, so we must ensure that we have a user-friendly design and process of building a deck. Magic The Gathering is a diverse hobby offering many different game types and sets of cards that many people may enjoy, therefore our users may expect our service to fulfill their needs for their specific format or needs. Users may view their decks by a deck list, titled ‘My Decks’ on the homepage, to see their collection of built decks.


## Features

<!-- Outline the core functionalities of your application and explain how they will work.-->

<!-- Provide a concrete example demonstrating how the application fulfills its purpose.-->

o	The Features our group plans to implement, at the moment, are a search function for cards and a deck builder. Our search function will allow users to find cards based on their mana cost, color identity, name, and even the set they belong to. The deck builder will accommodate the prime formats of the hobby and allow users to build, maintain, and update their favorite decks with any cards they wish to add or swap. For example, let’s say that a user is wishing to build a deck for the titular character from Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, the newest set released by Wizards of the Coast, Leonardo, one of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. However, our user doesn’t know which of the Leonardo cards that Wizards of the Coast printed in the set, so, our user searches for Leonardo cards using our search function. The user then selects their favorite card from the results and chooses to make a deck featuring this card. The user then can add other cards to maximize the synergy of their deck.


## Data

<!-- Describe the types of data that users will create, read, update, and delete (CRUD).-->

<!-- Include an example of this data using either real or representative sample values.-->

	This application is based upon fulfilling the user’s need to create and organize their decks. The users will create decks, specifying which format they are playing, building them to their liking. The users can read about cards in our search function, to help them find cards that would synergize with their decks. The decks can be updated at any time, either adding or removing cards. The user can delete a certain deck if wanted, removing it from their ‘My Decks’ section on the homepage.
